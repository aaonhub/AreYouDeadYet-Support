Text Alerts: SMS Terms

Effective date: September 27, 2026

The program

Are You Dead Yet text alerts are safety texts for the emergency contacts of people who use the Are You Dead Yet check-in app (also shown as "Dead Man's Switch"). An app user checks in on a schedule. If they miss a check-in, the contacts who signed up for texts get an alert, and a follow-up when the user checks in again. The app user can also send a test text. We never send marketing or promotional texts.

How you sign up (opt in)

Only the person who will get the texts can sign up. An app user can't sign you up.

  • The app user sends you a personal sign-up link or code from their own phone. We do not text you at this point.
  • On the web: open the link, enter your own mobile number, and tick the consent box, which is never pre-ticked. We then send one confirmation text. Reply YES to finish. If you don't reply YES, you get nothing more. Example page: dms.aaon.pro/join/DEMO.
  • By text: text JOIN and your code (for example, JOIN K7Q2XD4M) to our number. We reply to confirm.

What you'll get, and how often

Message frequency varies. You'll get a text only if the person misses a check-in, when they check in again after an alert, or if they send you a test. That is usually 0 to 2 messages a month. Message and data rates may apply.

Help and opting out

  • Reply STOP (or STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT) to any text to stop all texts from us, for every app user who added you. We'll send one message confirming you've opted out. Reply START to resubscribe.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email areyoudeadyetapp@gmail.com.
  • Reply NO to a confirmation text to decline that one request.

The fine print

  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Texts are available in the US and Canada.
  • Consent to texts is not a condition of any purchase.
  • This is not an emergency service. If someone may be in danger, call 911 or your local emergency number.
  • We may change or end the program. We'll update these terms, and the texts themselves always say how to opt out.
