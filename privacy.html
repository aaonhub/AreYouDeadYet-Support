Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

This policy covers the Are You Dead Yet app (also shown as "Dead Man's Switch" and "Are You Dead? Deadman Switch"), its server, and its text-message alerts. The app is made by an independent developer (Aaron Jin), called "we" below. Questions: areyoudeadyetapp@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

There is no account or login. The app creates a random ID for you, and our server stores only what it needs to run your switch:

  • Your switch: the name you enter, your check-in interval, your check-in times and history, your time zone, and the message you write for your contacts.
  • Your emergency contacts: the names and email addresses you enter. If a contact signs up for text alerts, we also store the mobile number they gave us and a record of their consent (when and how they agreed, and the exact wording they agreed to).
  • Notifications: a push-notification token so we can remind you before your deadline.
  • Purchases: if you buy Pro, we store the App Store transaction ID and when Pro ends. Apple handles all payment details; we never see them.
  • Server logs: basic request logs (such as IP addresses), kept for security and troubleshooting.

We do not collect your location, contacts list, photos, or health data. The app has no advertising and no third-party analytics.

How we use it

Only to run the service: to track your deadline, remind you, and alert the contacts you chose (by email, and by text if they signed up) if you miss a check-in, and to tell them when you check in again. We do not send marketing, and we do not sell or rent any information.

Text messages (SMS) and mobile numbers

Text alerts go only to people who signed up themselves on our sign-up page, or by texting JOIN and a code to our number, and who then confirmed. See the SMS Terms.

  • We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data and consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • The text-message originator opt-in data and consent is never shared with any third party, except the service providers that deliver the texts for us (below).
  • Reply STOP to any text to opt out. We keep a record of the opt-out so we never text that number again unless you reply START.

Who processes data for us

We use a few service providers, only to deliver the service and under their own privacy terms: Turso (database hosting), Resend (sending emails), Twilio (sending and receiving text messages), and Apple (push notifications and in-app purchases). They may not use the data for their own purposes. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays while you use the app. Deleting your data in the app (or emailing us) deletes your switch, contacts, check-in history, and your contacts' text-alert sign-ups from our server. A contact's STOP record is kept, so we don't text them again. Server logs are kept for a limited time.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access limited to the developer. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we store to the minimum.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.

Contact Us

Questions about privacy, or want your data deleted? Email areyoudeadyetapp@gmail.com.
