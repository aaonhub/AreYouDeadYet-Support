How It Works

  • Set up contacts - Add up to 3 emergency contacts who will be notified
  • Write your message - Compose the message your contacts will receive
  • Arm the switch - Enable the dead man's switch
  • Check in regularly - Tap the check-in button before your timer runs out
  • If you don't check in - Your contacts will be automatically emailed

Common Questions

What happens if I miss a check-in?

If you don't check in before your timer expires, the app will automatically send your pre-written message to all your emergency contacts via email.

How do I change my check-in interval?

Go to Settings in the app and adjust the check-in interval to anywhere from 1 hour to 30 days.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Your data is stored securely on your device using encrypted storage. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
